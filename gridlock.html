3Shops and malls want customers and goods. Offices and factories want workers, and factories need raw materials trucked in on the highway at the map's edge. Nobody wants to live in factory smoke or beside a highway.
4Busy junctions jam. Add roundabouts, lights, lanes or overpasses, and use Tune to set light timings, protected left turns and lane arrows.

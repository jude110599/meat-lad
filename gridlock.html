GRIDLOCK

A traffic city builder. Draw the roads, keep every shop, office and factory busy, and don't let the town jam up.

1Drag anywhere to draw a road. It snaps onto other roads, junctions and buildings, and straightens out the wobble in your line.
2Every week brings new houses, shops, offices and factories, and nothing appears unless you put it down. Place them with Build, drag them around to move them, or knock them down with Erase.
3Houses join any road that runs right past them and send their cars to wherever people are needed. Shops want customers and goods, offices and factories want workers, and factories truck goods to the shops. Nobody wants to live in factory smoke.
4Busy junctions make cars take turns. Add roundabouts, traffic lights, wider roads or an overpass to keep things moving.

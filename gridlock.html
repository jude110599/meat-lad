A traffic city builder. Draw the roads, keep every shop, office and factory busy, and don't let the town jam up.

How to play
1The map starts empty. Place your first shop and houses with Build, then drag roads to join them up, or lay a whole grid in three taps.
2Every trip grows the town. Each level brings more homes, apartments, shops, malls, offices and factories to place. Drag to move them, or knock them down with Erase.
3Shops and malls want customers and goods. Offices and factories want workers, and factories need raw materials trucked in on the highway at the map's edge. Assembly plants need parts from a warehouse. Nobody wants to live in factory smoke or beside a highway.
4Busy junctions jam. Add roundabouts, lights, lanes, one-way streets or overpasses, or run a highway with on and off ramps, and use Tune to set light timings, protected left turns and lane arrows.
